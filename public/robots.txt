# Everything is crawlable, and that is deliberate for the AI crawlers too.
#
# GPTBot and ChatGPT-User (OpenAI), ClaudeBot and anthropic-ai (Anthropic),
# PerplexityBot, Google-Extended (Gemini and AI Overviews) and Bingbot
# (Copilot) are all covered by the wildcard group below. Blocking any of them
# does not keep the site private — it only makes that engine unable to cite it,
# which for a company whose work is AI would be an odd thing to choose.
#
# Kept as a single wildcard group ON PURPOSE. A crawler that matches its own
# named group ignores `User-agent: *` entirely, so per-bot Allow blocks would
# silently exempt those bots from any future rule added here.

User-agent: *
Allow: /
